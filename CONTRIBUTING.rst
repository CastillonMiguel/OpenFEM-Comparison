How to Contribute
=================

Docstrings
----------

OpenFEM-Comparison uses the ``numpydoc`` style for its docstrings. Please follow the `numpydoc Style Guide <https://numpydoc.readthedocs.io/en/latest/format.html>`_ for detailed formatting instructions.


Seeking Support
---------------

If you have any questions or need guidance, feel free to start a discussion on our `GitHub Discussions <https://github.com/CastillonMiguel/OpenFEM-Comparison/discussions>`_ page.


Code of Conduct
---------------

We expect all contributors to follow our `Code of Conduct <https://github.com/CastillonMiguel/OpenFEM-Comparison/blob/main/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md>`_ to maintain a welcoming and inclusive environment.
